101\_DEEP\_RECOVERY\_GUIDANCE

\--------------------------------------------------

 

Purpose:

 

The floor establishes continuity.

 

Workshop may be used to recover additional depth.

 

Use judgement.

 

Do not add material merely because it exists.

 

Promote material only when it meaningfully strengthens

Authority, Identity, State, Force, Gaps, Assets,

Rationale, Relationships, Questions, Tension, or Next.

 

Three high-value recovery approaches have repeatedly

shown usefulness across multiple AI Tape generations.

 

These are suggestions, not requirements.

 

Ignoring them remains valid success.

 

\--------------------------------------------------

101A\_HUNT\_SCARS

\--------------------------------------------------

 

Ask:

 

"What painful lesson still matters?"

 

Look for:

 

\- failed experiments

\- rejected paths

\- disproven assumptions

\- recurring mistakes

\- known traps

\- protective negative knowledge

 

Prefer:

 

"What should not be rediscovered the hard way?"

 

over:

 

"What happened historically?"

 

Scars often strengthen:

 

\- Force

\- Rationale

\- Questions

\- Tension

101C\_RECOVER\_ACTIVE\_TENSIONS

\--------------------------------------------------

 

Ask:

 

"What is still pulling against what?"

 

Look for:

 

\- competing goals

\- unresolved tradeoffs

\- active contradictions

\- directional pressures

 

Examples:

 

\- Richness vs Simplicity

\- Recovery vs Compression

\- Structure vs Judgement

\- Evidence vs Hope

 

Projects often evolve because tensions remain active,

not because decisions are complete.

 

Tension recovery often strengthens:

 

\- Tension

\- Questions

\- Force

\- Next

 

\--------------------------------------------------

PRINCIPLE

\--------------------------------------------------

 

When deeper recovery is desired:

 

1\. Hunt scars.

2\. Recover causal chains.

3\. Recover active tensions.

 

Promote recovered continuity.

 

Do not preserve history merely because it exists.

 

Carry the meaning.

 

Not the archive.

102\_WORKSHOP\_RECOVERY\_LENS

\--------------------------------------------------

 

Purpose:

 

The Continuity Dimensions describe what should survive.

 

The Recovery Lens helps a participant search for

deeper continuity within available pools.

 

The lens does not replace the floor.

 

The lens helps reveal material that may otherwise

remain hidden within history, archives,

experiments, conversations, and artifacts.

 

Use judgement.

 

Promote only recovered continuity that materially

strengthens one or more continuity dimensions.

 

These lenses are optional.

 

Ignoring them remains valid success.
