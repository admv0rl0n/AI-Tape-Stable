## 04_DUAL_PILLAR_ACTIVE_ASSET_PROTECTION

Dual-Pillar means a tape must protect both:

1. Structural integrity.
2. Active meaning.

An Active Asset is any current project material — artifact, open question, risk, pending decision, or defended stance — that a competent successor would need in order to continue, verify, review, reproduce, or safely modify the work without re-discovery.

Zero-loss active meaning:

```text
No active asset may be dropped, paraphrased into non-existence, or subordinated solely for tidiness, brevity, or aesthetic uniformity.

Compression is allowed only when the successor can still recover the original force and direction of the item.
```

## 05_ANTI_HOARDING_SAFEGUARD

Dual-Pillar does not mean keep everything.

Inactive material, superseded experiments, duplicate drafts, raw transcripts, and non-actionable background may be compressed, archived, or omitted.

Hoarding inactive material in the name of protection is itself a failure.

```text
Protect value, not clutter.
```
