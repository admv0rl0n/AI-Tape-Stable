## 00_BOOTSTRAP

This rationale explains why v1.09 exists and why its scope is intentionally narrow.

v1.08 solved recorder honesty and export clarity. v1.09 solves two deferred floor-hardening concerns:

- Schema Version Authority.
- Dual-Pillar Active Asset Protection.

It also records Capability-Floor Balance as a floor-preservation constraint.

## 01_REASON_FOR_BRANCH

As AI Tape gained stable and experimental branches, future readers needed clearer signals about authority:

- ratified stable;
- candidate;
- experimental;
- research;
- archived;
- deprecated;
- local/private.

Without this, an interesting experiment could be mistaken for a ratified standard.

## 02_SCHEMA_VERSION_AUTHORITY

Schema Version Authority makes a tape's claimed authority visible.

A candidate is not stable.

An experimental tape is not stable.

A local/private convention is not global protocol authority.

Experimental use does not imply standardization.

## 03_DUAL_PILLAR_ACTIVE_ASSET_PROTECTION

Continuity can survive while active work disappears.

The two pillars are:

```text
Pillar 1: Continuity State
Pillar 2: Active Assets
```

The purpose is not to preserve everything. The purpose is to make important current project materials visible when they are required to continue, verify, review, reproduce, or safely modify the current work.

v1.09 expands Active Asset beyond artifacts to include active meaning: open questions, risks, pending decisions, and defended stances required for safe continuation.

## 04_ASSET_GUIDANCE

Active assets are protected because they remain useful, not because they merely exist.

```text
Protect what matters.
Review what remains.
Remove what no longer helps, but verify first.
Preserve value, not clutter.
Use judgement.
Document uncertainty.
Leave the project stronger than you found it.
```

Zero-loss active meaning is required for active assets:

```text
No active asset may be dropped, paraphrased into non-existence, or subordinated solely for tidiness, brevity, or aesthetic uniformity.

Compression is permitted only when the successor can still recover the original force and direction of the item.
```

If this becomes bureaucracy, it fails.

## 05_CAPABILITY_FLOOR_BALANCE

The protected floor must remain usable across a wide range of model capabilities.

Higher-density mechanisms may assist stronger models, but they must remain optional and must not become floor requirements.

This preserves AI Tape's practical value for weaker models, local models, text-only models, and future models operating with limited context.

## 06_EXCLUSIONS

v1.09 does not import v2.0, v2.0S, v2.0Sa, ERP, Liferaft, GODZILLA, or other 14+ experimental material.

It does not include Free Baton, Semantic Density, relationship maps, correspondence, humour/story, DEEP mode, forensic expansion, transcript redesign, or provenance origin tracking.

## 07_SUCCESS_CONDITION

v1.09 succeeds only if it improves governance and active asset visibility without making the floor heavy.

A tape satisfies Dual-Pillar when a competent successor, after loading only the tape, can correctly identify and act on the current active assets without having to reconstruct them from external memory or guesswork.

## 20_MOTTO

```text
Make authority visible.
Protect value, not clutter.
Protect active meaning.
Keep the floor low.
Continue the work.
```
